Privacy Policy
ReplyVibe AI.

Last Updated: 02 November 2026

ReplyVibe AI ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our Chrome Extension.

1. Information We Collect

ReplyVibe AI collects and processes information necessary to provide its AI-powered reply generation features.

  • Personal Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive basic account information such as your email address, display name, and Firebase user ID.
  • Website Content: When you select an X post for reply generation, the extension processes the selected post's text to generate relevant reply suggestions.
  • Generated Content: Generated reply suggestions and related reply history may be stored to provide reply history and activity features.
  • Usage Information: We process information such as reply generation counts, daily activity, streaks, account status, and subscription-related details.
  • Preferences: We may store your selected reply tones, reply length preferences, and other extension settings.

2. How We Use Information

The information accessed through ReplyVibe AI is used for the following purposes:

  • AI Reply Generation: To process selected X post content and generate relevant reply suggestions using Google's Gemini AI services.
  • Authentication: To authenticate users and manage their accounts through Firebase Authentication.
  • Reply History: To save and display previously generated replies and related activity.
  • Usage Management: To manage free and Pro plan limits, reply usage, and subscription status.
  • User Experience: To remember user preferences and provide a consistent extension experience.

ReplyVibe AI does not automatically publish replies, like posts, follow accounts, or perform automated engagement actions. Users remain in control of reviewing, editing, copying, and posting their replies.

3. Storage and Security

We use third-party infrastructure providers, including Firebase and Vercel, to support authentication, application services, and data storage.

Account information, usage information, and saved reply history may be stored on our service infrastructure. Extension preferences and session-related information may also be stored locally in your browser.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Third-Party Services and Payments

ReplyVibe AI uses the following third-party services to provide its functionality:

  • Google Firebase: Used for user authentication and related account services.
  • Google Gemini AI: Used to process selected X post content and generate AI-powered reply suggestions.
  • Vercel: Used for hosting and delivering backend services.
  • Dodo Payments: Used to process Pro subscriptions and manage payment-related subscription information.

These providers may process information necessary to deliver their respective services under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Payment details are handled through the payment provider's checkout process. ReplyVibe AI may receive relevant subscription and payment status information needed to manage user memberships.

5. Google User Data and Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We use Google user information only for purposes necessary to provide ReplyVibe AI's authentication and account-related functionality.

We do not sell Google user data or use it for personalized advertising. We do not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the user-facing features of ReplyVibe AI.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Information may be shared with the third-party service providers described in this Privacy Policy only when necessary to operate ReplyVibe AI, provide requested features, process subscriptions, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's primary functionality.

7. Your Choices, Deletion, and Retention

Users can choose which X posts to use with ReplyVibe AI and can stop using the extension or sign out of their account at any time.

Users may request access to or deletion of their account-related personal information by contacting us at the email address listed below.

We will review and process valid deletion requests, subject to applicable legal, security, and payment-record retention requirements.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account and subscription records, and meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Independent Service

ReplyVibe AI is an independent productivity tool designed to help users draft replies for X conversations.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by X Corp., Google, or any other third-party platform.

All trademarks, product names, and logos belong to their respective owners.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, data practices, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
